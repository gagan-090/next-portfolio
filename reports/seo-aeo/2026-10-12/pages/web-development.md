**How much does websites & web apps cost?**

**How long will the first release take?**

I estimate delivery after reviewing the workflow and dependencies. The milestone plan identifies required approvals, integration access and what you can test at each stage.

**Can we work entirely by email and WhatsApp?**

Yes, I handle freelance project enquiries and written project updates through email and WhatsApp. Send a project brief; no call booking is required.
