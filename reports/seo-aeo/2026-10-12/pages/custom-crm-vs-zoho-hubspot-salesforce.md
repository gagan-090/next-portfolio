### Start with an off-the-shelf CRM, unless one of these is true

Zoho, HubSpot and Salesforce are excellent products. For a small team with a standard sales pipeline, they are usually the right first step. A custom CRM starts to make sense when the tool begins to shape your business instead of the other way around.

### Signs you have outgrown an off-the-shelf CRM

Per-seat licences are growing faster than revenue as you add telecallers, field agents or franchisees.
Your team keeps a parallel spreadsheet because the CRM cannot model your process (site visits, quotations, service tickets, dealer networks).
Field staff need a simple mobile app that works offline, not a complex desktop dashboard.
You need deep integration with your own systems: ERP, billing, inventory, WhatsApp or a customer app.
Customer data must be hosted in a specific region or stay fully under your control.

### What a custom CRM typically includes

Lead capture from website forms, ads and calls, with automatic assignment.
Custom pipeline stages that match your real sales process.
Mobile apps for telecallers and field agents, with call logging and visit check-ins.
Role-based access for admins, managers and agents.
Automated follow-up reminders via push, email or WhatsApp.
Dashboards for conversion, team performance and revenue.

I built exactly this kind of system with TMConnect, a Flutter CRM for telecallers and field agents, and an HRMS & CRM app published on both app stores.

### A quick build-vs-buy checklist

Will licence costs over the next 3 years exceed a one-time build plus hosting?
Is your sales process a competitive advantage that generic tools flatten?
Do you have field or mobile-first users?
Do you need to own your data and integrations end to end?

If you answered yes to two or more, a custom CRM, often built in phases starting with the most painful workflow, is worth a written scope review.

### FAQs

**How long does a custom CRM take to build?**

I assess this after reviewing your workflow and dependencies. Scope, milestones, ownership and any external costs are agreed in the written proposal.

**Can I migrate data from Zoho or HubSpot?**

I can assess migration after reviewing the available exports or API access. The scope defines contacts, deals, history, field mapping and reconciliation checks; not every record is automatically exportable.

**Do custom CRMs have mobile apps?**

They can. Companion Android/iOS apps for field teams are one of the main reasons businesses go custom.

**Can I discuss this project without a call?**

Yes, send your project brief by email or WhatsApp. I use written questions and milestone reviews to clarify the scope.

**What should I send to get a useful quote?**

Send the users, essential workflow, required platforms, integrations and any existing code or data. Mention your launch dependencies and budget range if known.

**Are the prices and timelines fixed packages?**

No, I quote the actual scope and dependencies. The proposal separates development, third-party costs, acceptance checks and ongoing support.
