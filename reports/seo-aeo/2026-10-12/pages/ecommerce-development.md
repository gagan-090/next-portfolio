### How I scope your e-commerce project

I trace an order through payment, fulfilment, cancellation and refund before deciding the first release. I need your catalogue source, inventory rules and payment-provider requirements. A multi-seller marketplace needs separate agreement on seller permissions and settlement responsibilities. I avoid quoting it as a simple storefront with extra screens.

### What determines price and delivery

I quote the agreed scope after reviewing the users, essential features, integrations and existing code or data. The written proposal separates development from recurring services and lists milestones, acceptance checks and handover terms. I do not publish an unconfirmed starting price or promise a launch date before that review.

I estimate delivery after reviewing the workflow and dependencies. The milestone plan identifies required approvals, integration access and what you can test at each stage.
