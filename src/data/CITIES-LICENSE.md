# City data attribution

Source: GeoNames, https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip and https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/admin1CodesASCII.txt

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Snapshot downloaded: 12 October 2026. GeoNames provides the data as-is without guarantees of accuracy, timeliness or completeness.

Adaptations: filtered to India, South Africa and USA, populated-place feature codes; resolved region names; selected 6,000 records by population; created ID-based URL slugs; retained place names, region, IANA zone, coordinates and GeoNames ID. Population is used for selection and is not published as a current population estimate. This is not an exhaustive list of all cities in those countries.

Selection and source fields are documented in scripts/import-cities.py. Attribution and individual source record links are rendered in the directory.
